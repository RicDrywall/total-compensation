For comparing a real offer

Real compensation

Cash you can spend, benefits that replace spending, minus insurance premiums and the care costs you expect to pay. A deductible is not pay, and it is not a guaranteed loss.

Sample numbers are filled in so you can see the math. Replace them with your offer. Nothing leaves this browser.

    The offer

    Cash

    Bonus and extra cash are what you reasonably expect, not a guarantee.

    Retirement

    Only the match the employer adds. Your own contribution is still your money.

    Health

    Employer premium and HSA are added. Your premium and expected care costs are subtracted. The deductible itself is not.

    Other amounts the employer adds

    Count equity as the average that vests per year, not the whole grant.

    Time off

    Shown as days. Dollars are added only if unused PTO is paid out.

    Year 1 only

    Kept off the ongoing number so a signing bonus does not make every future year look richer.
