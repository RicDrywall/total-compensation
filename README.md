# Real compensation

A single-page calculator for what a job is actually worth.

**Ongoing value** = gross base + expected bonus and other cash + employer match + flat deposit per paycheck + employer percent of gross deposited in your account + employer health premium + employer HSA + stipends + other pension deposit this year + annual equity vest + ESPP discount you will use + unused PTO that is paid out − your insurance premiums − expected medical out-of-pocket.

Signing bonuses and relocation are added only to the year-1 total.

## What is left out on purpose

- The deductible. It is a note, not pay and not a guaranteed cost. Expected spending is what gets subtracted. There is a switch to use the out-of-pocket max for a worst year.
- Your own 401(k), 457, 401(a), HSA, or FSA contribution. That money is still yours. A contribution percent is used only to figure a match.
- An account balance. The $59,599 already in a PERF account is savings, not this year's pay.
- A pension plan's composite employer rate. Indiana PERF's 11.2% mostly funds the system. It is not a deposit in your account. Count the 3% only when the state pays it and does not deduct it from your gross pay. The Hoosier START match is $15 per paycheck ($390 a year on 26 checks), deposited in the 401(a) because you contribute to the 457.
- PTO and holidays, unless unused PTO is paid out. Those days are listed, not priced.
- Employer payroll taxes, unemployment, and workers’ comp. You never receive them.
- A grant you have not been given.

Open `index.html` in a browser. Nothing is uploaded. Your entries stay in this browser.

Nothing here is tax, benefits, or employment advice. Bonus and equity figures are only as good as the numbers you type.
